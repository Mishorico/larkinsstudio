Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · larkinsstudio.com

This policy explains how Larkinsstudio collects and uses your personal information. It is kept simple and honest — just the things you actually need to know.

Who we are

Larkinsstudio is an independent cross stitch pattern design studio run by one person. Our website is larkinsstudio.com. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at: hello@larkinsstudio.com

What information we collect

We only collect information you provide to us directly:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or free pattern
  • First name — optionally, when you subscribe, so we can address you personally
  • Payment information — when you purchase a pattern. We do not store payment details ourselves — this is handled by Tribute

We do not collect any other personal data and we do not use cookies for tracking.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the free pattern you signed up for
  • To send you our newsletter with new patterns, updates and offers (only if you subscribed)
  • To deliver your purchased digital pattern by email

We will never sell your data, rent it, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

To run our store, we work with a small number of trusted services:

  • beehiiv — email newsletter platform. Stores your email and name for sending newsletters. beehiiv Privacy Policy
  • Tribute — payment processing. Handles all payments securely. Tribute Privacy Policy
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (sends your purchased pattern to your inbox)
  • Vercel — website hosting

How long we keep your data

We keep your email in our newsletter list for as long as you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your data is removed from our active list. Purchase records may be kept for up to 2 years for accounting purposes.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time — every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@larkinsstudio.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. We do not store payment details — all payments are processed by Tribute directly.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions: hello@larkinsstudio.com
